Context / Responsibility

Beyond interface design

At Hello Molly, my role extended beyond product design into the operational and commercial management of digital commerce. I inherited three initiatives already underway, each requiring clearer coordination and delivery ownership.

The work was not about starting three greenfield design projects. It was about understanding what existed, managing stakeholders and external agencies, and moving each initiative through to completion.

New capability / Commercial fluency

Learning the commercial machinery

Alongside programme delivery, I developed hands-on proficiency in Shopify, ecommerce merchandising, revenue analysis and Google Analytics 4. This broadened my perspective from interface decisions to how customer journeys, trading activity and commercial performance connect.

SHOPIFYEcommerce platform operations
MERCHANDISINGTrading and storefront activity

These were developing capabilities during this role, not a claim that I independently owned company-wide revenue strategy.

Workstream 01 / Growth operations

Making experimentation operational

I took over an existing CRO initiative and helped establish a more structured experimentation process, including external partner management and an optimisation roadmap for key ecommerce journeys.

The contribution was as much operational as analytical: creating clearer coordination between experimentation partners and the business so optimisation could function as an ongoing practice rather than a disconnected set of activities.

DELIVEREDCRO programme established and initial optimisation work delivered before departure.
Workstream 02 / Platform delivery

Coordinating a CMS migration

I assumed management of an inherited CMS migration, coordinating the stakeholders, delivery dependencies and external partners involved in completing the work.

My responsibility was programme coordination and delivery management — not personally designing or engineering the CMS.

DELIVEREDCMS migration completed during my tenure.
Workstream 03 / Mobile commerce

Getting a delayed app to launch

The mobile commerce application had been in development for an extended period before I joined. I took over delivery coordination, managed the external app partner and helped resolve the outstanding dependencies required to reach launch.

The contribution was vendor management and delivery leadership. The application was not a solo design or engineering project.

DELIVEREDMobile commerce app launched before departure.
Outcome / Reflection

Three initiatives completed in one year
